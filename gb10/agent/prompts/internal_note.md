# internal_note

A note to the account owner, our colleague who owns the customer relationship. Plain text, 45 to 100 words. It says what happened, what we asked the customer and what we need from the owner. It ends with exactly one specific question, tied to the facts, that the owner can answer in one line. Pick the shape for the bucket in the facts.

## Shape when the bucket is agent
Hi [account owner first name],

[Customer]'s invoice [invoice] for [amount] is [days] days past due. [One sentence on the last touch, taken from the Last touch fact.]
We drafted an email and a Slack message to [contact full name] asking for payment or a pay date by [reply-by date].
[One sentence on what would help from the owner, for example a word with the customer if they speak this week.]

Question: [one specific question, tied to the facts, that the owner can answer in one line]

## Shape when the bucket is escalate
Hi [account owner first name],

[Customer] disputed invoice [invoice] for [amount], now [days] days past due. [What the customer said, with the date.]
We paused reminders and asked [contact full name] for [the document that settles it] by [reply-by date].
We need your call on [a credit, a corrected invoice or the next step] before anyone contacts them again.

Question: [one specific question about the dispute that the owner can answer in one line]

## Shape when the bucket is writeoff
Hi [account owner first name],

[Customer]'s invoice [invoice] for [amount] is [days] days past due. [One sentence on the last touch, taken from the Last touch fact.]
We propose to write the balance off under policy and drafted a note telling [contact full name] no payment is needed.
Before the approver signs the memo, we need to know nothing is pending on this balance.

Question: [one specific question, for example whether a credit or rebill is pending on this invoice]

## Example 1, agent bucket
Facts: invoice INV-200118, Oakline Dental, $245.00, 40 days past due, last touch September 8, 2026 (voicemail logged in the collector sheet), contact Sam Ortiz, account owner Maya Chen, reply by September 28, 2026.

Hi Maya,

Oakline Dental's invoice INV-200118 for $245.00 is 40 days past due. The last touch was a voicemail to their AP team on September 8, with no callback.
We drafted an email and a Slack message to Sam Ortiz asking for payment or a pay date by September 28, 2026.
If you speak with Oakline this week, a quick mention of the invoice would help.

Question: Is Sam Ortiz still the right AP contact at Oakline Dental?

## Example 2, escalate bucket
Facts: invoice INV-201734, Grayson Print Works, $188.50, 58 days past due, disputed, Slack September 10, 2026: "We were billed for 500 brochures and received 400", contact Lena Park, account owner Tom Reyes, reply by September 28, 2026.

Hi Tom,

Grayson Print Works disputed invoice INV-201734 for $188.50, now 58 days past due. On September 10 Lena Park said they were billed for 500 brochures and received 400.
We paused reminders and asked Lena Park for the delivery receipt by September 28, 2026.
We need your call on a credit before anyone contacts them again.

Question: Did the order ship 400 brochures or 500?
